# DR-0004 · TOM v0.4.1: no natural person; positions only

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV), in conversation with `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` (the decider is a position)
- **Source:** the direction of GOV of 15 October 2026: in the AION system a natural person does not exist; every human exists as a legal persona, a position; the link between positions and natural persons belongs only to top management, in its own mechanism, and has nothing to do with the AI parts (translated). Proposed for the 49GAION Standard as §1.1, item 9, by `49GAION/DR-0013`.
- **Relation:** a wording change to the closed v0.4.0; no rule, stage, gate, test or card changes. DR-0001 to DR-0003 stay as merged.

## Decision

1. **Positions only.** Every human position this AIOC deals with, its own (the TOM Design Lead) and the organisation's (the Requesting Authority, the Cells that sign listed types), is a HOAC position, a legal persona. The blueprint and its documents name positions only.
2. **Occupancy is top management's.** Who occupies a position, and how, is top management's own mechanism. No part of this AIOC holds, reads or infers it. The rule that the Design Lead position and the Requesting Authority's position are never occupied by the same natural person is given to that mechanism; this AIOC never checks it.
3. **What changes.** Blueprint §2.1 (the TOM Design Lead position), §3 (its row, and a note), §14 and the version to v0.4.1; SL-A2 in `docs/INTAKE-WITH-SOURCE-LIFECYCLE.md`, which now addresses a position through its HU port, with the tool gateway checking the sign-in.
