# DR-0001 · The Target Operating Model AIOC: the second stage, from documents only

- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Decided by:** the direction given to `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` for this draft (the decider is recorded in the private holders register). This is a decision on this repository only, not a decision of 49GAION.
- **Recorded by:** `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`
- **Status:** in force for this repository's draft; the blueprint stays `draft` until its fixed test has run

## Decision

1. **A second AIOC.** The design of an organisation's target operating model is one AIOC, `[ORG]AIOC-TOM`, written on the 49GAION AIOC Blueprint Template. It is proposed to the 49GAION Library and Operating Protocol as the stage after Source Lifecycle (`upstream/`). It is placed there only by a pull request merged by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION, recorded as a decision record of the 49GAION hub.
2. **The earlier capabilities join it.** Request reconstruction, commission decomposition, entity work packaging, single-entity operating-model design, cross-entity assembly and the operational walkthrough become its Pods. Requester stewardship has already moved into Source Lifecycle.
3. **Documents only.** It reads only from Source Lifecycle, by reference, and asks no person anything about the organisation. Spoken words cannot be the basis of an operating model, because its output is the operating model of an organisation, a company, or even one person's work.
4. **Asking goes through Source Lifecycle.** What the sources do not answer, it asks of the Chief of Staff of `[ORG]AIOC-SL`. That Chief of Staff gets it as a document, an explainer or another approved source, and answers by reference.
5. **Only Source Lifecycle for now.** In later versions it may read from equivalent sources (Protocol §13, item 7).
6. **Its own repositories.** Its full text lives in its own writing repository; its code, its PRD and the earlier chain's code and records live in its code project. The Protocol and the Library point to the writing repository.
