# DR-0006 · TOM v0.6.0: what the model also states, and the organisation as it runs today

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Decided by:** the governing seat (GOV), in conversation with `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` (the decider is a position)
- **Source:** the directions of GOV of 15 October 2026 (translated): "Our TOM must also take, as an input, the capability of observing the organisation that runs now; if there is none, it stays empty"; "What can be given to Process and Position Design, give to it; what can be given to TOM, give to TOM; what remains, we must build"; and "Where a capability's real home is in our earlier AIOCs, add it there too", on a study of comparable products whose features were placed stage by stage. And the direction of GOV of 16 October 2026 to go on in the agreed order, so that the blueprints, engineering and documents of the human cycle are complete (translated).
- **Relation:** builds on DR-0001 to DR-0005; it supersedes nothing. It answers blueprint §13, H-07, which held observation of real work as not admitted.

## Decision

1. **The strategy frame (S1).** The model states the organisation's strategy on one page: purpose, values, long-range target, three-year picture, one-year goals with their measures, critical numbers with their definitions. Each element is cited from a held source, or asked once through EX-10, or `not given`. It is decided in one non-delegable row; purpose and values stay the listed type `set-purpose-or-values` where no source in force states them. A missing element never blocks the run. No element is ever written or inferred by this AIOC (blueprint Annex H.1).
2. **Delivery model and make-or-buy (S5).** Each capability carries how it is delivered, `in-house`, `shared service`, `outsourced`, `partner` or `not decided`, and the make-or-buy that follows, by code. No provider is named. A capability not delivered in-house names the crossings that carry it, checked again at S6 (H.2).
3. **Critical value streams (S5).** A stream is critical only on a cited reason from a closed list: revenue, customer, legal or regulatory, safety, continuity (H.3).
4. **Maturity (S5).** Each capability carries its level now, only from a held source, and its target, only from a held source or a decision; otherwise `unknown` and `not set`. Maturity is never of a role, a position or a person (H.4).
5. **Outside parties' journeys (S6).** For each counterparty entry, the moments where the party meets the organisation, in order, each a crossing already declared. Nothing is designed on the party's side; a moment no crossing carries is a seam finding (H.5).
6. **The value case (S8).** Per outcome of the frame: its measures, baselines and targets, compiled by code from the decided parts and decisions, with no figure of this AIOC's own; decided in one row (H.6).
7. **Priority (S8).** Each element row carries a priority, `must`, `should` or `could`, proposed by a fixed rule and decided with the element; the decided priority is recorded on the decision (H.7).
8. **Records of the work as it runs today (S0, S1).** This answers H-07. The input exists: exports of records of the work, by role-type and process, admitted in Source Lifecycle only as the organisation's projection, after an impact assessment and a data-protection decision (HF-17). An export that still says who did the work is refused, `person-level records`. The activity profile is compiled by code, or reads `no observation`, and the run goes on. This AIOC never watches work; the label `observed` stays unused (H.8; H-07, H-40).
9. **The organisation dictionary (S1).** Where Source Lifecycle serves one, it is read as a held source; a term is used in the organisation's own meaning (H.9).
10. **Nothing weakened.** No stage, gate, review point or seat is added or removed. Four release parts are added (twenty in all), each required.
11. **What changes.** Blueprint header and one-page, §0, §1, §2.2, §4.3, §4.4, §5.0, §5.1, §5.5, §5.6, §5.8 (RP1, RP5, RP6, RP8), §5.9, §9, §12 to §14, Annexes B, C, D and E (E.1, E.2), and Annex H (new); `docs/OUTPUT.md` (§4a, §7.7, §10a, §11a, new); `docs/CARRIED-RULES.md` §4.1, §4.5, §8.2, §8.3, §12.1, §12.2; `docs/DESIGN-BASIS.md` §9a; `docs/ASKING-CATALOGUE.md` (EX-10, EX-11, §4.3, §4.4); `docs/INTAKE-WITH-SOURCE-LIFECYCLE.md` (SL-A13; the v0.6 sockets contract); the v0.6 contracts `tom-release`, `input-pack`, `needs-request` and `needs-answer`, with their examples; adapter `ADP-SL` v1.1.0 and its fixtures; `tools/check_contracts.py` and `tools/release_lint.py`; TEST-TOM v5 (cases 109–123); the cards.
12. **To the Library.** The generalised features are proposed to 49GAION with this version, as `49GAION/DR-0023` and the matching Protocol text; they reach 49GAION only by pull request merged by its governing seat.
