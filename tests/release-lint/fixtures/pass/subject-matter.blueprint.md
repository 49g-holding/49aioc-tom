# Blueprint: the overhaul workshop

## Capabilities

<!-- el:CAP-1 -->
Receive aircraft engines from customers under contract, record their condition on arrival, and agree the scope of work with the customer.

<!-- el:CAP-2 -->
Strip, inspect, repair and rebuild customer engines, and release each one to service with its certificate.

## Decision rights

<!-- el:DR-1 -->
The workshop manager decides the order in which work is taken in. The quality manager decides whether a rebuilt unit is released to service.
