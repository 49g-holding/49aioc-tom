# Decision Package

## What is being decided

<!-- el:EL-01 -->
The finance unit owns the monthly close. It must be able to close the books within five working days, approve payments up to its delegated limit, and report to the board each quarter.

<!-- el:EL-02 -->
The finance unit hands approved invoices to the treasury unit each business day. Treasury pays them and confirms each payment back to finance.

## What stays unknown

- Who approves payments above the delegated limit when the board does not meet that month. The sources are silent; it was asked and carried as an unknown.

## Your choices

For each element: accept, correct (in your own words) or reject. Then one final status for the whole release. Silence decides nothing.
