# Role register: the insurance brokerage

<!-- el:ROLE-1 -->
**Insurance agent.** Decides which policy to offer a client, within the product rules. Accountable for client advice. Sends signed proposals to the underwriting lead; receives quotes from the underwriting lead.

<!-- el:ROLE-2 -->
**Underwriting lead.** Decides whether a risk is accepted, and on what terms. Accountable for underwriting. Sends quotes to the insurance agent; receives signed proposals from the insurance agent.
